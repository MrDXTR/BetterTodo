# Workspaces

Workspaces allow teams and organizations to group related boards, manage centralized team membership, and govern access across projects.

## Overview

A workspace acts as an umbrella organization for boards and collaborators:
- **Board Organization** – Group project boards by department, team, or client.
- **Centralized Membership** – Add team members once at the workspace level.
- **Workspace Navigation** – Switch between workspaces instantly using the sidebar selector or mobile header switcher.

---

## Workspace Roles & Permissions

| Role | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control over the workspace, including renaming, member management, transferring ownership, and deleting the workspace. |
| **Admin** | Can rename the workspace, invite new members by email, and manage roles of standard members. Cannot delete workspace or transfer ownership. |
| **Member** | Access to boards in the workspace according to board-specific permissions. Cannot alter workspace settings or invite other workspace members. |

---

## Workspace Settings (`/workspaces/$workspaceId`)

Workspace owners and admins can manage their workspace by clicking the settings gear in the workspace panel or navigating directly to `/workspaces/$workspaceId`.

### Key Capabilities

1. **Rename Workspace**
   - Update the workspace display name with real-time saving and validation.

2. **Invite Members by Email**
   - Add new collaborators directly using their registered email address.
   - Choose their starting workspace role (**Admin** or **Member**).

3. **Role Management**
   - Promote members to Admin or demote Admins to Member.
   - Owners can transfer workspace ownership to another active member.

4. **Member Removal**
   - Remove members from the workspace with immediate revocation of workspace access.

5. **Workspace Deletion**
   - Strictly reserved for the workspace Owner.
   - Displays a confirmation dialog showing associated boards before executing deletion.

---

## Assigning Boards to Workspaces

- **Board Creation** – When creating a board, select the destination workspace from the dropdown. If you are already inside a workspace, it is preselected automatically.
- **Board Settings** – Move boards between workspaces at any time from the board's **Settings Modal** under the **Workspace** tab.
