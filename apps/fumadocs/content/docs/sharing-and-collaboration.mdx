# Sharing & Collaboration

BetterTodo provides seamless sharing mechanisms for both internal teammates and external collaborators, backed by real-time reactive synchronization and granular role permissions.

## Board Roles Hierarchy

Each board member is assigned one of four distinct roles:

| Role | Badges | Permissions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Crown icon | Full control over the board, settings, invitations, member roles, and board deletion. |
| **Admin** | Shield icon | Can manage lists, cards, board settings, labels, custom fields, and invite new members. Cannot delete the board. |
| **Member** | Users icon | Can create and edit cards, check off checklist items, post comments, and upload attachments. Cannot modify board-level settings. |
| **Viewer** | Eye icon | Complete read-only access. Can view board columns, cards, checklists, and comments, but cannot modify, move, or delete any content. |

## Universal Share Links

Board Admins and Owners can generate shareable invite links directly from the **Board Members Panel**.

### Role-Specific Invite Generation
- When generating a link, choose the target role:
  - **Member Share Link** – Grants write permissions upon joining.
  - **Viewer Share Link** – Grants read-only access upon joining.
- A secure, unique token is generated and attached to a shareable link: `/invite/:token`.

### Public Invite Acceptance Flow (`/invite/$token`)
The invite route supports both existing users and brand-new visitors:
1. **Authenticated Users**:
   - Immediately view the invitation card displaying the board title, inviter details, and granted role.
   - Click **Accept & Join Board** to be added to the board and redirected directly to the board view.
2. **Unauthenticated Visitors**:
   - See an invitation preview banner prompting them to sign in or sign up.
   - The invite token is retained through the authentication flow, automatically enrolling them upon successful login or registration.
3. **Invalid or Expired Invites**:
   - Displays clear contextual error states with navigation links back to the home page or dashboard.

## Transactional Email Invitations

In addition to universal share links, board owners can send direct email invitations:
- Powered by [Resend](https://resend.com).
- Fully HTML-escaped and sanitized email templates protect against injection attacks.
- Secure, tokenized join links embedded directly in the email body.

## Real-Time Collaboration & Activity

- **Reactive State Sync** – All board modifications (card moves, title edits, comments, checklist updates) update instantly across all active clients via Convex live subscriptions.
- **Card Assignments** – Assign active board members to specific cards. Only authorized board members can be assigned.
- **In-App Notifications** – Notification center popover tracks card assignments, due date reminders, and board invitations with unread counters and safe relative links.
- **Threaded Comments** – Team members can leave timestamped comments and discussion notes inside any card's inspector modal.
