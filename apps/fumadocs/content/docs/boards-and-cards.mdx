# Boards, Lists & Cards

BetterTodo organizes work with Kanban-style boards, lists, and cards, designed with modern design engineering principles and tactile micro-interactions.

## Structure Overview

- **Board** – Top-level workspace container. Features a title, description, color theme, visibility (Private, Team, Public), and workspace assignment.
- **List** – Column within a board (e.g., To Do, In Progress, Done). Can be reordered, duplicated, and animated on creation.
- **Card** – Work item within a list. Managed via a modern 2-column inspector modal with inline editing, attachments, checklists, and custom fields.

---

## Boards

### Dashboard & Aesthetics
- **Dynamic Gradient Mesh Headers** – Board cards display multi-stop gradient meshes derived from the board's color theme with a subtle 3-column kanban silhouette watermark.
- **Role Badges & Metadata** – Cards display role indicators (Crown for Owner, Shield for Admin, Users for Member), visibility pills, and relative update timestamps (`Updated X ago`).
- **Live Search & Filter** – Instant live search filter by title or description with a single-click clear button.
- **Interactive Create Tile** – Dashed "+ Create new board" tile directly inside the boards grid for fast creation.

### Batch Deletion & Management
- **"Manage Boards" Mode** – Toggle batch mode on the boards page to select multiple owned boards at once.
- **Select All Owned** – One-click selection for all boards you own.
- **Bulk Delete Modal** – Confirmation dialog displaying the count and list of selected boards before permanent deletion.
- **Direct Card Menu Deletion** – Delete individual boards directly from the board card menu without entering batch mode.

---

## Lists

### Column Creation & Animations
- **Spring Pop & Glow** – Newly created lists enter with a dynamic spring pop animation (`list-spring-pop`) and an accent glow pulse (`list-glow-pulse`) matching the board's theme color.
- **List Duplication** – Duplicate any list along with all its cards, checklists, and labels in a single click.
- **Drag Handles & Reordering** – Reorder columns horizontally with dedicated `GripVertical` drag handles for reliable touch and mouse interaction.

## Cards & 2-Column Inspector

### Modern 2-Column Architecture
Card details are presented in a 2-column inspector architecture:

1. **Left Primary Workspace**:
   - **Edge-to-Edge Cover Banner** – Displays uploaded cover images or presets.
   - **Inline Editable Title** – Click to edit directly. Press `Enter` to save or `Esc` to cancel.
   - **Markdown Description** – Rich text description with inline editing and markdown preview. Press `Cmd+Enter` (Mac) or `Ctrl+Enter` (Windows) to save.
   - **Checklist Trees** – Subtasks with completion checkboxes and automatic progress bars.
   - **Threaded Activity & Comments** – Comment stream with timestamps and author avatars.

2. **Right Inspector Sidebar (270px)**:
   - **Mark Complete Pill** – Tactile toggle (`CheckCircle2` / `Circle`) with emerald status feedback and active scale response.
   - **Priority & Due Date** – Priority levels (Low, Medium, High, Urgent) and compact date formatting (`MMM d, yyyy`) with overflow prevention.
   - **Custom Fields Section** – Rendered when custom fields exist; automatically hidden when none are configured.
   - **Attachments** – File uploads and cover image controls.
   - **Card Actions** – Copy card, duplicate, move, archive, and delete.

### Custom Fields
Boards support custom field definitions across 5 distinct data types:
- **Text** – Short single-line text inputs.
- **Number** – Numeric values with validation.
- **Date** – Calendar date picker.
- **Select (Dropdown)** – Custom option lists.
- **Checkbox** – Boolean toggles.

Each custom field includes an informative tooltip describing the field type and requirements.

### Drag & Drop System
- Powered by `@hello-pangea/dnd`.
- **Portal Rendering** – Dragged cards portal to `document.body` with a fixed width (`268px`) to prevent coordinate desyncs and overflow clipping.
- **Dedicated Drag Handles** – Explicit `GripVertical` grab handles ensure smooth dragging on both desktop and mobile touch devices.
- **Cross-Board Boundary Protections** – Backend enforces that cards can only move between lists belonging to the same board, and target lists must not be archived.

## Viewer Mode (Read-Only)

Users with the **Viewer** role experience a complete read-only board:
- Board title, color, and settings cannot be altered.
- List creation, reordering, duplication, and deletion are disabled.
- Cards cannot be dragged, moved, edited, or deleted.
- Checklists, comments, and custom fields are visible in read-only mode without mutation controls.

---

## Automation Rules

- **Due Date Reminders** – Configure board-level reminder lead times (e.g. 24 hours before due date).
- **Background Cron Engine** – Scheduled Convex cron runs every 15 minutes to evaluate upcoming deadlines and dispatch in-app notifications.
- **Deduplication** – Reminders track sent states to prevent duplicate alerts.

---

## Board Import & Export

- **JSON Export** – Export the entire board structure (lists, cards, labels, custom fields, checklists) into a structured JSON file.
- **JSON Import** – Import backup JSON files to instantiate a new board with all assets and relationships accurately restored.
